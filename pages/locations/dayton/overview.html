Signs Your HVAC System Needs Servicing

Common Symptoms
Inconsistent Temperatures

Hot and cold spots from room to room often indicate airflow issues, failing components, or a system that needs maintenance.

Air & Odor Issues
Weak Airflow

If vents produce weak air, it could be a clogged filter, duct restriction, failing blower, or another blockage that needs service.

Noise
Unusual Noises

Banging, rattling, squealing, or hissing can point to loose parts, motor problems, or refrigerant issues that require attention.

Smell
Unpleasant Odors

Musty, burning, or chemical smells may indicate microbial growth, electrical problems, or contaminants in the system.

Efficiency
Increased Energy Bills

A sudden rise in energy costs without a weather change usually signals the system is working harder than it should.

Age
System Age

As equipment passes 10+ years, wear impacts reliability and efficiency. Frequent repairs are a sign it may be time to plan for replacement.
