The Source of Indoor Air Quality Problems

Dirt & Dust

Particles in the Air

The dust in your home creates an environment for bacteria, viruses, and pollutants to thrive—affecting your air quality.

Chemicals

Household Pollutants

Construction and household chemicals release compounds that harm air quality. Gas-burning appliances also release carbon monoxide.

Moisture

Mold & Mildew Growth

Too much moisture causes mold spores to enter the air, triggering allergies and respiratory problems.
