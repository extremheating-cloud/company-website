Common Signs You Need to Clean Your Duct System

  • Increased allergies & respiratory issues. Dust and allergens inside the system can trigger symptoms.
  • Visible dust & debris. Dust on vents or around the system often indicates ducts need cleaning.
  • Unpleasant odors. Musty smells may suggest mold or debris buildup in your ducts.
  • Dusty home or workplace. Frequent dust and clean surfaces that get dirty fast are red flags.
  • Increased energy bills. A dirty HVAC system works harder to maintain temperatures.
  • Inconsistent airflow. Some rooms feel uneven—there may be blockages.
  • Mold growth. Any mold around ducts or the HVAC system requires immediate cleaning.
