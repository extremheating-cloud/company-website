Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Extreme Heating & Cooling LLC and Extreme Home Services LLC collect when you use this site or schedule a visit, who it goes to, how long it is kept, and how to ask for a copy of it or have it deleted.

Effective August 3, 2026
THE SHORT VERSION

What Extreme collects, and what happens to it.

Extreme asks for what it needs to get a technician to the right address with the right parts, and nothing more. This page names every outside company involved rather than describing them generically, so you can see exactly where what you type ends up.

  • Booking a visit means giving Extreme your name, mobile number, service address and the details of the job. Email is optional.
  • Photos you upload are stored by Cloudinary, and anyone who has the link can open them, so upload only what you are comfortable sharing.
  • What you type into the address field is sent to Google, or to Komoot when Google is unavailable, to suggest matching addresses.
  • Extreme does not sell your personal information, and never shares mobile numbers for anyone else's marketing.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted.
  • Financing applications are submitted on the lender's website and are covered by the lender's privacy policy, not this one.
Note: This site does not take payment card numbers, bank account numbers or Social Security numbers, and it does not process payments. Please don't enter any of them in the notes field.

Who This Policy Covers

The companies. This Privacy Policy applies to extremeheating.com and to the information collected by Extreme Heating & Cooling LLC and Extreme Home Services LLC. In this policy, "Extreme" means both companies. Heating and cooling work is performed by Extreme Heating & Cooling LLC; plumbing work is performed by Extreme Home Services LLC. The two share this website, one phone number and one scheduling system, so one policy covers both.

Where Extreme operates. Extreme is an Ohio business serving homeowners in the Dayton and Cincinnati metro areas. Information collected through this site is stored and processed in the United States.

What this policy does not cover. This policy covers this website and the requests you send through it. It does not cover other companies' websites you reach from here, including lender sites, review sites and social media, each of which has its own privacy policy.

Information Extreme Collects

What you enter to schedule a visit. Your first and last name, your mobile number, your email address if you choose to give one, and the service address where the work will happen. You also tell Extreme how you would like to be reached, whether you have used Extreme before, what service you need and the follow-up answers you select, the day and arrival window you would like, and your acknowledgement of the booking terms shown to you before you confirm.

Optional details about your home. The details step is skippable. If you complete it, Extreme collects the property type, whether you own or rent, the approximate age of the system or the home, and where the outdoor unit sits. These help route the right technician with the right parts.

Anything you type in the notes field. The notes box is free text and goes to Extreme exactly as you write it. Please don't put payment card numbers, bank details, Social Security numbers or health information there. Extreme does not need any of it to schedule a visit.

Photos you upload. You may attach up to five photos of the problem. Photos are uploaded to Cloudinary, Extreme's image host, and Extreme receives a web link to each one. Those links are public: anyone who has the link can open the photo. Photos taken inside a home often show more than the equipment, so upload only what you are comfortable sharing. Photos are optional, and every request can be booked without them.

What you type in the address field. To suggest matching Ohio addresses, what you type into the service-address field is sent to an address lookup service as you type it, once you have entered at least three characters. See Service Providers for who that is.

What your browser sends automatically. Like most websites, this site and the services that deliver parts of it receive your IP address, your browser and device type, the pages you view, the page that referred you, and the date and time.

Advertising click identifiers. If you arrive from a Google Ads click, the site saves that click's identifier in your browser's local storage so a booking can later be matched back to the ad that produced it. It stays in your browser until you clear your site data and is not linked to you by name unless you go on to book.

What this site does not collect. This site does not take payment card numbers, bank account numbers or Social Security numbers, and it does not process payments or credit applications. Financing applications are submitted on the lender's own website, under the lender's privacy policy. See the Financing section of the Terms.

How Extreme Uses It

To do the work you asked for. Scheduling the visit, routing the right technician, confirming the appointment, reaching you if something changes, performing the work, and following up afterward.

To prepare before arriving. The issue you select, the details you give about your home, and any photos you upload are used to understand the problem before a technician arrives so the right parts are on the truck.

To keep your service record. Estimates, invoices, work performed and warranty coverage, so Extreme can honor the Limited Warranty in the Terms and answer questions about past work.

To administer the programs you join. X-Plan membership and Extreme Rewards referral credit, under the terms published on the Terms page.

To understand the website. Which pages and which ads lead to booked visits, so the site and the advertising spend can be improved. This is measured in aggregate, not to build a profile of you.

To meet legal obligations and protect people. Tax and business records, responding to lawful requests, investigating fraud or misuse, and enforcing the Terms.

Service Providers Extreme Uses

Extreme uses a small number of outside companies to run the website and the scheduling form. Each one receives only what it needs to do its job. They are named here rather than described generically, because you deserve to know exactly where what you type actually goes.

Formspree — form delivery. When you submit the schedule form, everything you entered, together with the links to any photos, is sent through Formspree, which delivers it to Extreme's inbox and keeps a copy in Extreme's Formspree account.

Cloudinary — photo hosting. Photos you attach are uploaded directly from your browser to Cloudinary and stored there. Extreme receives a link to each one. As noted above, those links are publicly accessible to anyone who has them.

Google — address suggestions, fonts and video. Google's Maps and Places service returns the address suggestions in the service-address field, and receives what you type there. Google Fonts serves the site's typeface on every page, and embedded YouTube videos load from Google when a page containing one is opened. Google receives your IP address in each case, and may set cookies if you play a video. Google's use of that information is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Komoot — backup address suggestions. If Google's address service is unavailable, the site falls back to Photon, a free geocoder operated by Komoot and built on OpenStreetMap data. The same address text is sent there instead.

jsDelivr — images. Photographs and graphics on the site are delivered by the jsDelivr content network, which receives your IP address.

Analytics and advertising measurement. Every page loads Google Tag Manager, which in turn loads Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads conversion tracking, and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel. These record that pages were viewed, that a booking form was opened and that a booking was completed, along with general device and location information, and they set cookies in your browser. Google and Meta receive that information directly. It is used to measure which advertising produces calls and bookings, not to identify you by name.

Call measurement. Google Ads may replace the phone number shown on the site with a forwarding number so that a call can be attributed to the advertising that produced it. The call still reaches the same office. The local numbers listed for each office are not replaced.

Booking and messaging widgets. Three further providers load on every page: Broccoli and Podium, which power the on-site chat and messaging, and the ServiceTitan online scheduler, which opens in a window on this site when you book. Anything you type into one of those is received by that provider as well as by Extreme. Their own privacy terms apply to what they hold.

When Else Extreme Shares Information

With the people doing the work. Your name, address, phone number and the job details go to the technician assigned to your visit and to the office staff scheduling and invoicing it.

When you direct it. If you ask Extreme to coordinate with a landlord, a property manager, a home warranty company or an insurer, Extreme shares what is needed for that purpose.

When the law requires it. To comply with a subpoena, court order or other lawful request, to establish or defend a legal claim, or where Extreme believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to prevent harm.

If the business changes hands. If Extreme is sold, merged or reorganized, customer records may transfer as part of that transaction. This policy would continue to apply to the information transferred until it is replaced by a policy you are notified of.

What Extreme does not do. Extreme does not sell your personal information, and does not give your name, phone number, email address or service address to other companies for their own marketing.

Calls and Text Messages

Why Extreme needs your mobile number. The number you give when you book is how Extreme confirms the appointment, tells you when the technician is on the way, and reaches you if the schedule changes. Choosing Call, Text or Email tells Extreme which you prefer.

Message rates. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies with your appointment.

Stopping messages. Reply STOP to any text message from (937) 744-7148 to opt out immediately, or call the office at (844) 584-7399 at any time and ask to be taken off text messages. Reply HELP to any text for assistance. Stopping texts does not cancel a scheduled visit: call the office to change or cancel an appointment.

Mobile numbers are never shared for marketing. Extreme does not sell or share mobile numbers, or consent to receive text messages, with any third party for that party's marketing purposes.

Cookies, Local Storage and Analytics

What this site stores on your device. The site saves advertising click identifiers in your browser's local storage, as described above, so that a booking can be attributed to the ad that produced it. Clearing your browsing data removes them.

Cookies set by others. Services embedded in the site can set their own cookies, most commonly Google, when an embedded YouTube video is played, and any analytics or advertising tags installed on the page. Extreme does not control those cookies.

Your browser controls. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and clear local storage, and most offer a private browsing mode. Blocking them will not stop you from booking a visit.

Do Not Track. Browsers vary in how they send Do Not Track signals and there is no common standard for honoring them, so this site does not respond to them.

How Long Extreme Keeps It

Service records. Records of work performed — estimates, invoices, equipment installed and warranty coverage — are kept for as long as needed to honor warranties, meet tax and accounting obligations, and answer questions about past work at your address.

Deletion on request. Whatever the periods above, you can ask Extreme to delete your information sooner, and Extreme will do so except where a record must be kept for warranty, tax, accounting or legal reasons. See Your Choices.

Your Choices, and How to Request Deletion

What you can ask for. You can ask Extreme for a copy of the information it holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for photos you uploaded to be deleted, ask to stop receiving marketing email or text messages, or ask for your information to be deleted altogether. Extreme honors these requests from any customer, wherever you live.

How to ask. Email info@extremeheating.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line, call (844) 584-7399, or write to Extreme Heating & Cooling LLC, 712 N Fairfield Rd, Beavercreek, OH 45434. Please include the name, phone number and service address used when you booked, so the right record can be found. That is the only reason Extreme asks for it, and it is not used for anything else.

What Extreme may have to keep. Some records cannot be deleted on request: invoices and tax records, records supporting an active warranty, and anything Extreme is required to retain by law or needs to establish or defend a legal claim. Extreme will tell you if that applies to your request.

No penalty for asking. Extreme will not refuse service, charge a different amount, or provide a different level of service because you exercised any of these choices.

A note on the third parties above. Deleting your information from Extreme's records does not by itself delete copies held by Google, Meta or other advertising services from their own tracking. Those are controlled through your browser and through your account settings with those companies.

How Extreme Protects It

Reasonable safeguards. The website is served over an encrypted connection, and form submissions and photo uploads travel over encrypted connections to the providers named above. Access to customer records inside Extreme is limited to staff who need it to do their jobs.

No guarantee. No website or method of transmitting information over the internet is completely secure. Extreme cannot guarantee absolute security, and any information you send is sent at your own risk.

What not to send. Please do not email or type payment card numbers, bank account numbers or Social Security numbers into the notes field or any other part of this site. Extreme will never ask for them there.

Children's Privacy

This site is for adults. Extreme's services are sold to homeowners and property owners. This website is not directed to children, and Extreme does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

If it happens anyway. If you believe a child under 13 has given Extreme personal information, contact info@extremeheating.com or call (844) 584-7399 and it will be deleted.

Photos of a home. A photo a customer uploads of a leak or a furnace may incidentally show other members of the household. Extreme uses those photos only to diagnose and prepare for the work requested, and deletes them on request like any other information.

Other Companies' Websites

This site links to websites Extreme does not control, including financing lenders, review platforms, manufacturer sites and social media pages. Following one of those links takes you out of this policy's scope. Anything you enter there is collected by that company, under its own privacy policy, not this one. That is especially true of financing: a credit application is submitted on the lender's site and Extreme never sees what you enter there.
