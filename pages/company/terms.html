Extreme Rewards Referral Program

Who offers the program. Extreme Rewards is offered and administered by Extreme Heating & Cooling LLC, including where the referred customer's job is performed by Extreme Home Services LLC.

What the program pays. Extreme Rewards pays an existing customer when a new customer they referred completes a qualifying job. The referred customer saves $250 on a new heating, cooling, or plumbing system, or $100 on everything else. The referring customer earns the same amount their referred customer saved, and Extreme issues it on a Visa gift card.

How a referral is credited. The referred customer must name the referring customer at the time they book the job. Extreme cannot apply a referral to a job after it is booked and does not accept retroactive claims.

Who qualifies as a referred customer. The referred customer must be new to Extreme, meaning Extreme has not performed service at that address within the previous 24 months.

How long a referral stays valid. A referral name remains valid for 90 days from the date it is given.

When rewards are issued. Extreme issues rewards on a Visa gift card after the referred customer's job is complete and paid in full, and within 90 days of that completion. Extreme does not pay on a lead, on a booking, or on an incomplete or unpaid job.

How many rewards can be earned. Extreme issues one reward per completed job. Where a single visit includes more than one type of qualifying work, Extreme issues one reward at the higher applicable amount. There is no limit on the number of separate referrals a customer may make.

Exclusions. Self-referrals and Extreme employees are not eligible. A property owner may not refer their own property.

Combining with other offers. The referral discount does not combine with other active promotions; the referred customer receives the greater of the two. Accrued X-Plan credit is not a promotion and applies in addition to the referral discount.

Taxes. Referral rewards may constitute taxable income to the recipient. Extreme may be required to collect tax information and to issue tax forms where reward totals meet applicable reporting thresholds. Recipients are responsible for any taxes owed.

X-Plan Membership

Who offers the membership. X-Plan is offered and administered by Extreme Heating & Cooling LLC, including member benefits that apply to work performed by Extreme Home Services LLC.

Pricing. X-Plan costs $249 per year or $20.75 per month.

Zero Risk Investment. 100% of the investment of an X-Plan membership in consecutive years is applied toward end-of-life equipment replacement, up to a maximum of $2,500 or 10 years, whichever comes first. A lapse in membership interrupts the accrual. The accrual follows the member, not the property, and does not transfer with the sale of a home.

Included services. Membership includes two Safety & Performance Visits a year, a multi-point air conditioner tune-up and service, refrigerant charge calibration up to 1 lb, a heating system safety checkup and service, detailed evaluation and efficiency measurements, airflow adjustments as needed, thermostat calibration and configuration, and professional recommendations to prolong equipment life.

Member benefits. Members also receive Extreme Priority Scheduling, 15% off all repairs, a reduced service fee including after-hours and holidays, a discount on the complete line of indoor air quality components including air duct cleaning, and access to exclusive member offers and discounts.

Repair warranty. Qualified repairs performed by Extreme technicians carry a Worry-Free 5-Year Warranty. It applies only to those repairs and remains subject to the exclusions set out in the Limited Warranty and No Warranty Items sections above.
